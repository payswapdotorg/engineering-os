Honest notes on tessellation:
- OCCT BRepMesh emits **per-face nodes** — vertices are duplicated across shared face edges (an axis-aligned box → 6 faces × 4 nodes = 24 verts / 12 tris). Counts are engine-pinned in the corpus manifest; changing the deflection pin or the engine pin must update the manifest together.
- STEP carries no in-file up-axis declaration; the adapter reports the OCCT canonical "z" (never rewritten — the viewer rotates). IFC's schema context is +Z up.
- The `has_occ=False` limitation carries over to IFC tessellation: curved surfaces are approximated by the wheel's default triangulation; the harness records the observed error per run and fails loudly beyond the pinned 1%.
- The PyPI wheel's metre-normalisation behaviour was verified empirically (probe file, not a claim from docs); a wheel that changes it would be caught by the metre-authored fixture volume cross-checks.
